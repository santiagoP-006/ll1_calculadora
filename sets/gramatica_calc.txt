# Gramática LL(1) — Calculadora Científica

Programa   -> Sentencia Programa | ε
Sentencia  -> id SentenciaP newline | Expr newline
SentenciaP -> assign Expr | TermP ExprP
Expr       -> Term ExprP
ExprP      -> plus Term ExprP | minus Term ExprP | ε
Term       -> Unary TermP
TermP      -> times Unary TermP | divide Unary TermP | mod Unary TermP | ε
Unary      -> minus Factor | Factor
Factor     -> sin lparen Expr rparen | cos lparen Expr rparen | tan lparen Expr rparen | abs lparen Expr rparen | Primary
Primary    -> number | id | lparen Expr rparen
